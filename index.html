The studio

Hi, I'm Izac. A storyteller and filmmaker based in Austin, Texas.

I picked up my first camera in 2016. In 2023 I launched Ike Media Productions, a full-service creative studio built around visual storytelling with purpose.

Since then I've worked across music, real estate, non-profits, beauty, health and fitness, automotive, weddings, and sports. Great storytelling is equal parts craft and connection, so every project gets both: a strategy for how the film will work, and the heart that makes people stay with it.
