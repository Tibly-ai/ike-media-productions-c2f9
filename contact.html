First call to final cut

How the work
actually goes.

Five steps, no mystery. You always know where the project stands, what is next, and who is holding it.

  1. 01

    The call

    Thirty minutes on goals, audience, and budget. We ask what the video needs to do for revenue, not just how it should look.

  2. 02

    Concept and quote

    A written treatment with the story angle, shot approach, deliverables, and a flat price. Nothing moves until you sign off.

  3. 03

    Pre-production

    Locations, crew, schedule, shot list, interview questions. You get a call sheet before the shoot day so there are no surprises.

  4. 04

    Production

    Film-grade camera, lighting, and sound on set in Austin or wherever the story lives. Calm room, clear direction, people at ease on camera.

  5. 05

    Edit and delivery

    First cut, your notes, color and sound, then final files cut for every place you plan to post them.
