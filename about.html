About the founder

Hi, I'm Izac.
A storyteller and filmmaker
based in Austin.

My journey into visual storytelling started with my first love: baseball. That passion led me to a scholarship at the Academy of Art University in San Francisco, where I earned my B.A. in Communications with a minor in Film in 2018.

I picked up my first camera in 2016. Seven years later I launched Ike Media Productions, a full-service creative studio built around visual storytelling with purpose.

I've worked across music, real estate, non-profits, beauty, health and fitness, automotive, weddings, and sports, bringing a blend of strategy and heart to every project. Great storytelling is equal parts craft and connection. Brand campaign, documentary-style piece, or social content, I work to make sure your story feels honest, cinematic, and effective.

The mission is simple: tell stories the best way possible, and help clients grow their brand and revenue through intentional content.
