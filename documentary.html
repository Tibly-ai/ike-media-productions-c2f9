The approach

Documentary work runs on trust. The camera comes second. I spend time with a subject before a single frame is recorded, so the interview sounds like a conversation instead of a script read.

Great storytelling is equal parts craft and connection.

A documentary piece usually starts with a long phone call. We talk about who the film is for, what it needs to do, and which moments actually carry the meaning. From there I build a shoot plan that leaves room for the unplanned, because that is where the best footage tends to show up.

On shoot days the crew stays small and quiet. Natural light where it works, sound treated carefully, and enough time to let people settle. In the edit, story order comes before pretty shots. The result is a film that holds attention and still feels true to the person in it.

Selected work

Formats I
shoot most

Every documentary project is scoped to the story rather than a package. These are the shapes the work usually takes. Private links to finished films go out with each proposal.

Request the reel
  1. 01

    Non-profit impact films

    Five to eight minute films built for donors, boards, and grant applications. Interviews with the people served, the volunteers who show up, and enough field footage to make the numbers feel real. Delivered with vertical and short-form cutdowns for social.

  2. 02

    Athlete profiles

    I played college baseball on scholarship, so training rooms and empty fields at 6am are familiar ground. These pieces follow the work behind the highlight: the reps, the setbacks, the family in the stands. Useful for recruiting, sponsorship decks, and personal brand.

  3. 03

    Human-interest portraits

    Single-subject shorts about a person, a place, or a craft. Shot over one or two days, cut around the voice, and paced to let quiet moments land. These travel well at festivals and on a brand's owned channels.

  4. 04

    Documentary-style brand stories

    The documentary method applied to commercial goals. Founder interviews, real customers, and the day-to-day of the business, edited with a sales structure underneath. If you want the polished campaign version, that lives on the branded content page.
